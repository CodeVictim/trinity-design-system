# Radio Button Field

A native radio button with a label, for choosing exactly one option from a small set. Built from the **Radio Button Input** atom. Figma: *Radio Button Field* `30173:78607`, 8 authored variants.

<InfoCallout>Accessibility pattern: <b>choice-controls</b> (Storybook A11y Library). Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.</InfoCallout>

## Accessibility

**Built in (the component handles it)**
- Role: native `<input type="radio">` inside a `<label>`, so clicking the text selects it and the label is the name.
- States: selected / not selected / disabled. Screen readers get these from the browser, plus the position ("2 of 3") when radios share a `name`.
- Keyboard: arrow keys and roving focus come from the browser for native radios — no JavaScript handlers (pattern pitfall).
- Hidden from AT: the drawn circle (Radio Button Input) is `aria-hidden`; the real input is invisible but sits over the 24×24 touch area.

### Focus

- Indicator: 2px outer ring (`--radiobutton-focus-outer-stroke`) around the circle, shown on `:focus-visible`. It snaps on — no transition.
- Ring vs page: 15.61:1 (Light) / 21:1 (Dark).

### Visual & pointer

<SimpleTable head={['Check', 'Light', 'Dark', 'Needs']} rows={[
  ['Label text vs page', '15.61:1', '21.00:1', '4.5:1'],
  ['Circle ring vs page (unselected)', '15.61:1', '21.00:1', '3:1 (1.4.11)'],
  ['Circle ring vs circle fill', '18.08:1', '16.44:1', '3:1'],
  ['Focus ring vs page', '15.61:1', '21.00:1', '3:1'],
]} />

- **Not color-only:** selected changes shape (the ring grows from 1px to 4px), not just colour.
- **Hover fill:** 1.85:1 against the page in Light, 7.06:1 in Dark; in Dark the white ring sits on it at 2.97:1. That's not a failure: the pointer itself shows hover, so an added hover treatment is supplemental and doesn't need 3:1 (W3C, <a href="https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html">Understanding Non-text Contrast</a>). The ring still reads 16.44:1 against the circle's own fill, and the focus ring is the cue that must pass.
- **Target size:** the touch area is 24×24 (meets 2.5.8), and the label adds to the clickable area.
- **Disabled** is exempt from contrast requirements (1.4.3).
- **Motion:** the Field handles it (Radio Button Input only defines the look): the hover fill fades and the selected ring grows over `--radiobutton-duration` (→ `duration/fast`, 150ms) with `--radiobutton-easing` (→ `easing/ease-out`), matching the Figma prototype. Focus snaps on. **Reduced motion:** the growing ring becomes instant; the colour fade stays.

## Usage guidelines

**Do**
- Always put radios in a group with a legend that asks the question.
- Stack them vertically, one per line.
- Pre-select the most likely option when there's a safe default.

**Don't**
- Don't use a single radio on its own — use a checkbox.
- Don't use radios for choices that take effect immediately — use a toggle.
- Don't hide the label unless the context makes it obvious, and even then keep `label` as the accessible name.

## Content guidelines

- Labels: sentence case, no trailing punctuation, short and parallel in form.
- The legend is the question ("How should we contact you?"); the labels are the answers.
